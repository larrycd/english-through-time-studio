English Through Time Studio 1.0.0 - by Larry Dalton

Translate between today's English and three older stages of the language:
Early Modern English (Shakespeare, the King James Bible), Middle English (Chaucer, Wycliffe)
and Old English (Aelfric, Beowulf).
- Translate both ways, word by word, with how each word was chosen and an honest rating
- Accuracy measured on Gospel verses the translator never learned from, shown against a baseline
- Write in five scripts: modern edition, Early Modern print (long s), blackletter, insular uncial,
  Anglo-Saxon runes; save SVG, PNG or JPEG and read your files back
- Old and Middle English dictionaries (about 125,000 Wiktionary entries with their forms)
- The New Testament in six versions side by side, from the Old English Gospels to a modern translation
- Pronunciation in IPA for each period, spoken aloud (eSpeak NG)
- Self-test page

Install: extract this folder, then double-click Install.cmd.
The studio installs for your Windows account only (no administrator rights), runs at http://localhost:8772/
and starts automatically when you sign in. The infographic is copied to your Desktop.

Uninstall: run Uninstall.ps1 from %LOCALAPPDATA%\EnglishThroughTimeStudio, or use Settings > Apps if you used the setup program.
